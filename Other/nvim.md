## Movement
h,j,k,l to move
w,b to move forward/back words
e to move to the end of a word
0,$ beg/end of line (^ for first char)
f then char, finds occurrence of that char (, ;), precede with number to find that nth char (applies to other keys as well)
gg, G move to top/bottom line
M to go to middle of page
zz takes current line to the middle
% takes to next bracket


## Changing Mode
esc, i to change from normal to insert 
I to  move to insert at the start of the line
a to move to insert forward 
A to move to insert at the end of the line
o to add new line below

## Deleting
dw delete word
use diw to delete in word (applies to other stuff as well, a is around)
dd delete line

## copy/paste/undo
u undo
ctrl R redo
p paste
P paste before cursor
yw yank word (copying)
yy yank line

## Visual Mode
v to enter
use movements to select then operator

## Replace Mode
R to enter

## Other
. repeat last thing

## Search
/ to enter Search
n, N to move forward
